Description

The observe module has two failing tests that reproduce identically on a clean tree, so they predate current work: a collector timeout and a retention test that is date-sensitive. A date-sensitive test is the more serious of the two because it fails by calendar rather than by code, which trains everyone to ignore a red suite and erodes the quality gate it exists to enforce. Fix the retention test to control its own clock rather than depending on the current date, and diagnose whether the collector timeout is a genuine race or an under-tuned bound.
