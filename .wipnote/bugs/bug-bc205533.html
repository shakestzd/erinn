Description

feat-d3bb4992 implemented the block-gate skip as a 'blocks_waiver: <reason>' marker parsed by regex out of decisions_notes free text, rather than a typed PlanSlice field. The choice was driven by scope (schema.go was outside the slice's three assigned files in a shared tree), not by design. research_waiver is already a typed field validated the same way, so the asymmetry is a wart: a regex over prose is weaker to validate, invisible to schema consumers, and easy to typo. Promote blocks_waiver to a real optional field mirroring research_waiver, keeping the marker readable for back-compat.
