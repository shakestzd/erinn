Description

Post-cutover serve starts the telemetry indexer in the background, immediately reports its handshake ready, and continues writing .index-offset files. The largest shard has a 4 MiB-per-500 ms hard lower-bound catch-up of roughly 36 seconds before parse/insert cost, so ready means listener-ready rather than telemetry-ready. This both violates the no persistent checkpoint boundary and exposes incomplete dashboard data without a health state.
