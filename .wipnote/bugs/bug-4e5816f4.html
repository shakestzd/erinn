Description

A full reindex on this corpus was observed taking roughly 70 minutes, and that was attributed to the per-commit git diff-tree subprocess storm in reindexFeatureFiles. That diagnosis is now measured and wrong about magnitude: benchmarked in isolation against the real corpus of 3549 feature-commit pairs and 3486 distinct commits, the pre-fix function took 45.8 seconds, not tens of minutes. The batching fix cut it to 0.81 seconds, a genuine 56x win on a real hot path, but it cannot account for the reported wall-clock. Something else dominates the pipeline. Profile the full reindex end to end and identify where the time actually goes before anyone attributes slowness to a specific stage again. Candidates worth timing first: the trailer scan, edge reindexing across all collections, HTML parsing of the full store, and per-item SQLite upsert patterns. Re-measure the end-to-end figure after the batching fix landed, since the baseline may also have shifted.
