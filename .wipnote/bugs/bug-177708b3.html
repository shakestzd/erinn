Amortize pruneMetricSignals: only prune when over the keep limit by a margin

Todo Medium Priority

Part Of:

  • trk-2c55adea

Description

Follow-up to bug-e764997c. The guard added there (only call pruneMetricSignals when the current WriteBatch call actually adds a metric-kind signal) cut one real session's prune-call count from ~86,000 to ~7,715 (its metric-signal count). But in steady state, once a session's metric row count sits at the 5000-row keep limit, EVERY new metric write pushes it to 5001 and re-triggers a full DELETE+ORDER BY/LIMIT re-sort of the session's entire metric set to prune back to 5000 -- measured directly at ~12.9ms/call even at steady state. For that one session, 7715 remaining prune calls x ~13ms is roughly 100 seconds of residual cost, on top of whatever the bug-e764997c fix already removed. Proposed fix: only prune when the session's metric count exceeds the keep limit by some margin (e.g. keep+500), then delete the excess down to the keep limit in one pass -- amortizing the expensive re-sort across many writes instead of paying it on every single one. Needs its own equivalence proof against the real corpus and a measured before/after, same standard as bug-e764997c. Not folded into that fix intentionally, per team-lead direction, to keep that commit small and provable.
