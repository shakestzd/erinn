Every claim written by a dispatched subagent lands under agent_id __root__ because WIPNOTE_AGENT_ID is unset in the subagent environment. Reproduced directly: a named teammate checked its own env, found the variable absent, ran feature start, and the claim landed as __root__. This is the actual blocker for correct attribution, not the receiver read path. Fixing the receiver to resolve per-signal agent identity is a no-op while there is nothing but __root__ rows to join against. Investigate where the launcher and hook handlers can obtain and propagate a stable per-agent identity.
