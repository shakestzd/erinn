Description

Chooser design bug across Claude, Codex, Gemini, and Antigravity: the 'Resume this session' row presents stale/current work-item labels as if they identify the session, even though sessions are CLI conversation/runtime identities, claims are mutable attribution, and worktrees are workspace continuity (often track/work-item keyed). This caused a completed/stale bug label (bug-2623b061) to appear under 'Resume this session' for Codex. Future fix should redesign chooser groups/labels/actions so session resume, work-item continue, and worktree/track workspace reuse are distinct concepts across all supported CLIs. Related lineage: plan-311440bf explicit-intent launch/resume chooser.
