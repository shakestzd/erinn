Description

Three compiled Go test binaries are tracked in git: db.test, hooks.test, and tmp/hooks.test. These are build artifacts (output of 'go test -c'), not source — they should not be version-controlled. They were discovered while fixing bug-2c9995a7 (the wipnote.test untracked-binary gitignore gap); their tracked status is why a broad '*.test' gitignore glob was unsafe there. Fix: (1) VERIFY each is actually a compiled test binary (run 'file' on them) and that nothing references them (e.g. a test or script that execs ./hooks.test, or a fixture) — git log --diff-filter=A to see when/why they were added; (2) if confirmed artifacts with no references, 'git rm --cached' them (untrack, keep on disk); (3) once untracked, prefer switching .gitignore to a broad '*.test' glob (now safe) and remove the narrow '/wipnote.test' rule added in bug-2c9995a7 — OR add explicit rules for each.
