DIAGNOSED 2026-08-09: silent data loss, live, affecting every current reader of otel_signals. Root cause is a UNIQUE index on otel_signals(span_id) in core/db/otel_schema.go combined with INSERT OR IGNORE in the receiver writer. At most one row can exist per span_id; every later signal sharing that span is discarded with no error. In OTel span_id identifies a span, not a signal, and Claude Code emits many logs against one enclosing interaction span. Measured: 86,758 of 155,855 ingestable lines dropped (92.6 percent of the gap). The remainder is the documented 5000-metric-rows-per-session prune, which is by design. Isolated repro reproduced the loss to the decimal with zero residual. Ruled out with evidence: counting artifact, ingest lag, frozen bookmark, shouldPersistSignal, retention. Survivors are biased toward whichever signal arrived first per span, so dashboard aggregates are not a random sample. Canonical NDJSON is intact and the loss is recoverable by re-ingest. Acceptance Criteria Row count matches ingestable line count minus the documented metric prune. Existing installs recover the lost rows rather than staying at their current checkpoint. Expected Behavior Every ingestable NDJSON signal line becomes exactly one otel_signals row. Signals sharing a span_id all persist, because span_id is not a signal identity.
