Description

Plans have become unreadable in practice - slices go unread because the document is the union of every field at one altitude, and the block catalog formats text (tables/lists) rather than encoding anything visually. Investigate where the bulk actually is across the plan corpus, what a real reader experiences, and design an information architecture fix: overview-first triage layer, progressive disclosure of slice detail, and a re-cut block vocabulary. Rendering technology is not the problem.
